CLAMP Privacy / Rev. 2026

Privacy

clamp.calyvent.com · 100% client-side

CLAMP runs entirely inside your browser. Your typography inputs are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server. There is nothing for us to see.

01 · No upload, no server

Every operation — calculating clamp functions using the linear slope formula, converting pixel values to rem units, generating CSS properties, mapping to Tailwind utilities, and rendering the live sandbox canvas — is performed locally using JavaScript executing in your browser. Your inputs do not leave your device.

02 · What we store

CLAMP does not use local storage or any persistence mechanism. All calculations are ephemeral and exist only during your session. When you close the tab, everything is gone.

03 · No accounts, no cookies, no tracking

There are no user accounts, no authentication, and no tracking cookies. We do not run third-party advertising or behavioural analytics on the tool. We cannot identify you.

04 · Third-party code

The application loads only its own first-party scripts. No external code executes against the contents of your inputs.

05 · Children

CLAMP is a general-purpose developer utility and is not directed at children under 13.

06 · Changes

If this notice changes, the revised version will be posted here. Because the tool stores nothing about you, changes only affect how the utility behaves on your device going forward.

07 · Contact

CLAMP is built and maintained by Velocity, a Calyvent studio. Questions may be directed there.
